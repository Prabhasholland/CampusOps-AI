NUST admission uses a point system based on final school-leaving results (NSSC, Cambridge, or GCE), converted onto NUST's Admission Point Scale — English is always counted, and Mathematical Literacy is not accepted where a programme specifically requires Mathematics. Every faculty has a general minimum (25, 30, or 37 points in five subjects, depending on the school), and most programmes add a specific subject-grade requirement on top of that. The Registrar's Office or Admissions can calculate a caller's exact score from their actual results — the figures below are what to check a caller's expectations against, not a substitute for that calculation.

### Faculty of Computing and Informatics

General minimum: 25 points in five subjects including an E-symbol in English on NSSC Ordinary Level. The Mature Age Entry Scheme is not accepted for this faculty, except for the Bachelor of Journalism and Media Technology.

- Bachelor of Computer Science — D-symbol in Mathematics on NSSCO, or E-symbol on NSSCAS.
- Bachelor of Computer Science in Cyber Security — C-symbol in Mathematics on NSSCO, or E-symbol on NSSCAS.
- Bachelor of Informatics — D-symbol in Mathematics on NSSCO, or e-symbol on NSSCAS.
- Bachelor of Journalism and Media Technology — D-symbol or better in English on NSSCO, plus a pass in a journalism-related test. Mature Age Entry is accepted with a minimum 50% pass rate in NUST's English Proficiency and Mathematics tests.

### Faculty of Engineering — School of Engineering

- Bridging programme (InSTEM), for applicants who don't yet meet the professional-degree entry criteria — 25 points in five subjects at Grade 11/NSSC Ordinary Level with a minimum C-symbol in Mathematics and Physical Science, or Chemistry and Physics; an E-symbol is required in English.
- Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degrees — 30 points in five subjects including Mathematics, Physical Science and English, with at least Grade 3/A-symbol on NSSCH or D-symbol on NSSCAS in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry — or a pass of the InSTEM bridging programme with a score of at least 50%.
- Professional Engineering Degree (B.Eng) — 37 points in five subjects including at least Grade 3 in Physical Science and Mathematics on NSSCH, or D-symbols on NSSCAS in Chemistry, Physics and Mathematics — or a pass of InSTEM with a score of at least 50%.

### Faculty of Engineering — School of the Built Environment

General minimum: 25 points in five subjects at NSSC Ordinary Level including an E-symbol in English, unless a programme states otherwise below.

- Diploma in Geomatics — Mathematics (C), Physics or Physical Science (D); preference given to applicants with at least a D-symbol in Geography.
- Diploma in Property Studies — Mathematics (E).
- Bachelor of Geomatics — 30 points in five subjects: Mathematics (A), Physics (B), English (B).
- Bachelor of Property Studies — 30 points in five subjects: Mathematics (E).
- Bachelor of Land Administration — Mathematics (E).
- Bachelor of Geoinformation Technology — Mathematics (C), Physics (C), English (C).
- Bachelor of Architecture — 30 points in five subjects: 12 points combined for English and Mathematics, plus 18 points from three of Arts and Craft, Biology, Economics, Fine Art, Geography, Literature, Physical Science, Technical Drawings, or Chemistry. No symbol lower than C is accepted.
- Bachelor of Urban and Regional Planning — 30 points in five subjects: 11 points for English and Mathematics combined (no symbol lower than C on NSSCO or e on NSSCAS), plus 19 points from three other non-language subjects (no symbol lower than D on NSSCO or e on NSSCAS). Mature Age applicants need 3 years of relevant work experience in planning.
- Bachelor of Quantity Surveying — 30 points in five subjects: 12 points combined for English and Mathematics, plus 18 points from three of Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Physics, Physical Science, or Technical Drawings. No symbol lower than C is accepted.
- Bachelor of Regional and Rural Development — 30 points in five subjects, using a combination of NSSCAS/NSSCH and/or NSSCO; no symbol lower than D in English and Mathematics on NSSCO.

### Faculty of Health, Natural Resources and Applied Sciences

General minimum: 25 points in five subjects including an E-symbol in English on NSSCO. Mature Age Entry is generally not accepted here unless a programme says otherwise.

- Bachelor of Science in Agriculture — E-symbol in Mathematics and Biology or a related science subject. Mature Age Entry accepted.
- Bachelor of Science in Horticulture — C-symbol in Biology or a science subject, D-symbol in Mathematics. Mature Age Entry accepted. (Both this and the Agriculture degree above are offered full-time at the Rietfontein site only.)
- Bachelor of Natural Resource Management — D-symbol in English and Mathematics, C-symbol in Biology, Geography, or Agriculture. Mature Age Entry accepted.
- Bachelor of Emergency Medical Care — under the NSSC old curriculum: A-symbol in Mathematics, Biology or Physical Science; under the new curriculum: A-symbol in Mathematics, Biology, Physics or Chemistry. Either way, a 60% score in English and 50% in Mathematics is required if sitting NUST's own entrance test.
- Bachelor of Medical Laboratory Sciences — old curriculum: 18 points in Biology, Mathematics and Physical Science, no symbol lower than C; new curriculum: 24 points in Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics and Physics, no symbol lower than B.
- Bachelor of Environmental Health Science — old curriculum: 15 points in Biology, Mathematics and Physical Science, no symbol lower than C; new curriculum: 20 points in Biology, Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, no symbol lower than C.
- Bachelor of Human Nutrition — old curriculum: 15 points in Biology, Mathematics and Physical Science, no symbol lower than C; new curriculum: 20 points in Biology, Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, no symbol lower than C at Ordinary Level or D at AS Level. Mature Age applicants need 50% in NUST's English and Mathematics tests.
- Bachelor of Applied Mathematics and Statistics — old curriculum: B-symbol in Mathematics at NSSC Ordinary Level or e-symbol at Advanced Level (a C-symbol under the old syllabus requires sitting an entrance test); new curriculum: B-symbol in Mathematics at Ordinary Level or E-symbol at Advanced Level.
- Bachelor of Science — old curriculum: 15 points in Mathematics, Biology and Physical Science, no symbol lower than C; new curriculum: 20 points in Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry and Physics, no symbol lower than C at Ordinary Level or D at AS Level.

### Faculty of Commerce, Human Sciences and Education

General minimum: 25 points in five subjects including an E-symbol in English on NSSCO.

- Bachelor of Accounting — 30 points in five subjects including a C-symbol in English and Mathematics or Accounting.
- Bachelor of Accounting (Chartered Accountancy) — 35 points in five subjects including a C-symbol in Mathematics and English.
- Bachelor of Economics — D-symbol in Mathematics.
- Bachelor of Business Management — D-symbol in Mathematics.
- Bachelor of Entrepreneurship — E-symbol in Mathematics.
- Bachelor of Public Management — E-symbol in Mathematics.
- Bachelor of Marketing — D-symbol in Mathematics.
- Bachelor of Procurement and Supply Chain Management — D-symbol in Mathematics.
- Bachelor of Transport Management — E-symbol in Mathematics.
- Bachelor of Communication — D-symbol in English.
- Bachelor of English and Linguistics — D-symbol in English.
- Bachelor of Business and Information Administration, Bachelor of Human Resources Management, Bachelor of Sport Management, Bachelor of Culinary Arts, Bachelor of Tourism Innovation and Development, Bachelor of Hospitality Management, and the Bachelor of Criminal Justice programmes have no subject requirement beyond the faculty's general 25-point minimum.

If a caller's programme isn't listed here, or they want the exact point total from their own results, that's a Registrar's Office / Admissions calculation, not something to guess at on the call.
