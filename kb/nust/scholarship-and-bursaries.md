# NUST Scholarship, Bursary, and Financial Aid Policy (2026)

## Institutional Scholarship Verification & Disbursement
All institutional scholarships, state financial aid (NSFAS/NASFAF), and external donor grants are administered through the Financial Aid Bureau located in the Administration Building (Ground Floor, Wing B).

### Verification Process
1. **Application Review**: Applications are processed in three batch cycles (February, May, September).
2. **Eligibility Requirements**: Students must maintain active registration with a minimum weighted academic performance score of 60% and submit all certified parental income affidavits.
3. **Disbursement Cycles**: Approved scholarship allowances are disbursed directly to student fee accounts on the 18th of each calendar month.
4. **Payment Clearance & Hold Resolution**: In cases where disbursement is delayed or marked blocked:
   - Missing tax affidavits require submission of original certified copies to Counter 4.
   - Bank clearance takes 48 hours following batch approval.
