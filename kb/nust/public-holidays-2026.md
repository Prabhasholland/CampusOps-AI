Administrative offices are closed on all Namibian public holidays, in addition to their normal weekend closure (see office hours). The confirmed 2026 dates: New Year's Day (01 January), Independence Day (21 March), Good Friday (03 April), Easter Sunday (05 April), Easter Monday (06 April), Workers' Day (01 May), Cassinga Day (04 May), Ascension Day (14 May), Africa Day (25 May), Genocide Remembrance Day (28 May), Institutional Holiday (29 May), Heroes' Day (26 August), Day of the Namibian Women and International Human Rights Day (10 December), Christmas Day (25 December), and Family Day (26 December).

A caller asking whether an office is open on a specific date should be checked against this list before assuming a normal working day.
