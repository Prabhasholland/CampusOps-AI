# NUST Academic Advising & Multi-Party Meeting Coordination Policy (2026)

## Multi-Party Advising & Panel Scheduling
Academic advising sessions involving students, faculty advisors, and departmental administration must be coordinated and registered in advance.

### Coordination Protocol
1. **Advising Windows**: Official faculty advising hours are scheduled on Tuesdays and Thursdays between 14:00 and 16:30.
2. **Venue Booking**: Department meeting rooms (such as FCI Building Room 302 and FEBE Block B Room 114) must be logged and reserved via the respective Department Administration Office.
3. **Prerequisite & Curriculum Review**: Students requesting prerequisite waivers or graduation audit meetings must bring their current academic transcript and syllabus summary.
