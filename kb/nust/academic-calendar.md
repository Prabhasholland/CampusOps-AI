The academic year runs in two semesters. Semester one typically begins in late January and runs through to early June, including a mid-semester break; semester two runs from July through November, ending with the main examination period. Exact dates shift slightly year to year and are published on the academic calendar on the university website — when a student asks for a specific date, it's more reliable to point them to that published calendar than to state a date from memory, since calendars are revised periodically.

Registration for each semester opens several weeks before teaching begins and has its own deadline — registering late is possible but usually carries a late registration fee, and in some cases needs the Registrar's Office's approval if it's very late.

Each subject has its own drop deadline within the semester, generally falling a few weeks after teaching begins, and its own assessment dates set by the department teaching it — there is no single "exam week" date that applies to every subject, particularly for subjects with practicals or continuous assessment instead of a single final exam.

Supplementary exams, for students who narrowly fail a subject's main assessment, are scheduled in a dedicated period after the main results are released, usually a few weeks into the following semester. Students are notified individually if they qualify for a supplementary rather than needing to check for themselves.

When a query is really about a specific date rather than a policy — "when do exams start", "when is registration" — and the exact date isn't something the agent can look up live, it's better to say the academic calendar will confirm the exact date than to guess.
