This covers currently-enrolled international students keeping their study permit on file with Academic Administration - not general visa advice for prospective applicants, and not something the agent should extend beyond what's below. Any specific visa or permit question - eligibility, how to apply, processing time - goes to the Registrar's Office; permit rules are individual and change without notice, and the KB does not cover application guidance.

International students are required to submit a copy of their valid study permit for Academic Administration's records. What's required depends on the student's situation:

- Windhoek-based students present both the original and a copy of their latest endorsed study permit.
- Students with permanent residence or a diplomatic visa submit both the original and a copy of their permanent residence certificate, diplomatic visa, and passport.
- Students currently outside Namibia email the contact below with their full name, student number, and the country they're currently in.

Two things are never valid proof of a study permit, regardless of how official they look: an approval letter or acknowledgement receipt from the Ministry of Home Affairs, Immigration, Safety and Security (MHAISS), and an Employment Permit or Temporary Resident Permit - unless the Immigration Selection Board at MHAISS has specifically granted a change of condition. A student who has only an approval letter still needs to apply and pay for the actual study permit, normally within three months of that approval letter.

Not complying with the study permit requirement can lead to deregistration.
