---
title: Subject cancellation
topic: subject_cancellation
office: registrar
---

Students can cancel, or "drop", a subject they are enrolled in, but only before that subject's drop deadline. Each subject has its own deadline, set at the start of the semester and shown on the student's registration record — it is not the same for every subject, so always check the specific subject's deadline rather than assuming a single date applies to the whole semester.

Before the deadline, dropping a subject is straightforward: the student confirms which subject they want to drop, and it is removed from their registration with no academic penalty. Depending on how far into the semester it is, dropping a subject may still carry a partial fee liability — students are not automatically refunded for a dropped subject, so it's worth explaining this plainly rather than letting them assume it's free.

After the deadline has passed, a subject cannot be dropped through the normal process. The student would need to either continue with the subject and sit its assessments, or apply for a late withdrawal through the Registrar's Office, which requires a documented reason (such as illness or a family emergency) and is not guaranteed to be approved.

When handling a cancellation request, always confirm the exact subject code the student means — students sometimes remember a subject by its common name rather than its code, and departments occasionally run subjects with similar names. Confirm the code back to them before proceeding.

If a student is unsure whether they're within the deadline, check their record rather than guessing, and if the deadline has already passed, be direct about it: explain that the standard drop window has closed, describe the late withdrawal option, and note that this will need to go through the Registrar's Office rather than being resolved immediately.
