title: Proof of registration
topic: proof_of_registration
office: registrar
---

A proof of registration is the official document confirming a student is currently registered for the academic year. It's most often needed for a student's bank, a scholarship or sponsor, an employer, or immigration purposes.

The document is generated automatically once two conditions are met: the student has completed registration for the current semester, and there is no outstanding balance on their student account. If either condition is not met, the system will not issue the document, even if the student has requested it.

If a student has an outstanding fee balance, the balance must be settled before the document will generate. Payment can be made at the Cashier's Office in the Admin Building, or by electronic funds transfer using the student number as the payment reference. Once a payment clears, the document is issued automatically within 24 hours — there is no separate approval step, so students do not need to request the document again after paying.

If a student's registration is incomplete — for example, they haven't finalised their subject selections or submitted a required document — the proof of registration cannot be issued until the Registrar's Office closes out their file. This is a manual step and can take a few working days.

Once available, students download the document themselves from the student portal, under the "My Documents" section. Staff cannot email it directly for security reasons; the student must retrieve it from their own portal login.

If a student says the portal isn't letting them download the document, the two most common reasons are an unpaid balance or an incomplete registration — check both before assuming it's a technical fault. If both are clear and the portal still isn't working, log it as an IT issue rather than a registration issue.
